About MDK Smart Shop

A Tokoza business serving the community since 2022.

Who we are

MDK Smart Shop is an online business that offers customers a wide range of services. We are currently serving the Tokoza community, and we started trading in 2022.

We sell household supplies. That includes grocery hampers for the kitchen and home, smart IoT devices and refurbished tech, and solar products such as back-up systems, batteries and panels.

Need something that is not in the catalogue? Send us a special order request and we will come back to you with options.

What we sell

Tech gadgets

Breakfast hampers, lunch hampers and home essentials hampers.

Solar products

Back-up systems, batteries and solar panels.

How we do business

Every order follows the same four steps, so you always know what comes next.

  1. You choose

    Add products from the catalogue to your quote, or ask us for something specific.

  2. We quote

    You receive a quotation with a quote number, your total and our banking details.

  3. You pay

    Pay by EFT with the quote number as your reference, or pay cash at our location.

  4. We hand over

    Once payment is confirmed we prepare your order for collection, or arrange delivery.

Our policies

Quotations and prices

Quotations are valid for 7 days. Prices come from our current price list and may change after a quotation expires. Stock is confirmed once payment is made.

Payments

We accept EFT and cash. For EFT, use your quote number as the payment reference and send us proof of payment. Cash payments are made at our location in Tokoza.

Collection and delivery

You can collect from 8679 Tshepo Street, Grinacker, Tokoza. If you need delivery, tell us when you request your quotation and we will confirm availability and cost separately.

Refurbished tech

Refurbished items are described honestly, including their condition. Ask us about warranty before you order.

Problems with an order

If something is wrong with your order, contact us as soon as you can with your quote number and we will work with you to put it right.

Your information

We use your name, phone number and email only to prepare quotations, reply to your enquiries and arrange your order.
